The stop is the angle the hinge or a stay should hold the cover at. It is shown in the build list.

Frame and hardware

Screws go along every fixed vertical edge into heat-set inserts in the posts, plus the base (and the top, when the front is the door). Max pitch is the largest gap between screws; 80–120 mm gives a stiff box.

Panel features

Vent slots are 6 mm wide, under the 12 mm finger-safe limit.

Test probe holes

Height is the centre of the pattern above the bottom edge of the panel. Across moves it from the panel centre (towards the front on the side panels). Holes that would hit an edge, a corner post, a vent, the notch or a hinge are left out and flagged in the build list.

Hardware

SVG and DXF are 1:1 in mm. Hinge and handle holes are M4 clearance (Ø4.5). The DXF puts each panel on its own layer (PANEL_BACK, PANEL_LID…), so you can import one panel at a time and extrude it to the acrylic thickness. The STL is the whole box, closed, in mm, for checking fit in an assembly.

STEP 3D is the one for SolidWorks 2025: real solids (flat faces, round holes) as an assembly with one part per panel, hinge, post, handle and switch, already in place. Open it in SolidWorks, then Save As .SLDASM - the parts save as .SLDPRT.
